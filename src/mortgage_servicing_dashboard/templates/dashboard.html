Methodology

Meaning travels with every value.

Servicing Lens is a presentation layer over the application’s governed public-evidence model.

01

Official evidence first

Filed SEC materials and official records outrank issuer copies. Search results and model recollection cannot support a number.

02

Exact arithmetic

Money, UPB, rates, growth, ratios, and presentation normalizations use Decimal in Python—never JavaScript financial math.

03

Missing stays missing

Customer loans, platform names, and sentiment remain unavailable where the governed pipeline has no field.

04

Mix semantics remain explicit

PFSI owned mix is owned-MSR UPB / total UPB. TFC shows bank-owned loans serviced / total UPB and is not described as MSR ownership.
